
## What are Retries?

Suppose a Producer sends an event.

```text
Producer

↓

OrderPlaced
```

But due to:

* Network issue
* Broker unavailable
* Temporary failure

Kafka doesn't acknowledge the request.

Instead of immediately failing,

the Producer **tries to send the same event again**.

This is called a **Retry**. 

---

## Why are Retries Needed?

Example:

```text
Producer

↓

OrderPlaced

↓

Network Failure ❌
```

Without retries,

```text
Event Lost
```

With retries,

```text
Producer

↓

Retry

↓

Kafka Receives Event ✅
```

The event is successfully stored.

---

## How do Retries Work?

```text
Attempt 1

↓

Failed

↓

Attempt 2

↓

Failed

↓

Attempt 3

↓

Success
```

Kafka Producer automatically retries according to its configuration. 

---

## Benefits

* Handles temporary network failures.
* Increases message delivery reliability.
* Reduces chances of losing events.

---

# Notes

* Retry means the Producer sends the same record again after a temporary failure.
* Retries are handled automatically by the Kafka Producer.
* Retries improve reliability during transient failures.
* The number of retries is configurable. 

---

# Remember ⭐

* **Retry = Try Again.**
* Retries handle **temporary failures**, not permanent ones.
* Using retries increases the probability that an event is successfully delivered.
