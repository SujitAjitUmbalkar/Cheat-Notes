
## What are Acks?

When a Producer sends an event to Kafka, it needs to know:

> **"Was my event stored successfully?"**

Kafka sends a confirmation back to the Producer.

This confirmation is called an **Acknowledgement (Ack)**. 

---

## Why are Acks needed?

Example:

```text
Producer

↓

OrderPlaced
```

Suppose the network fails.

The Producer doesn't know:

* Was the event stored?
* Or was it lost?

An **Ack** tells the Producer whether Kafka successfully received the event.

---

# Ack Modes

## 1. `acks = 0`

```text
Producer

↓

Send Event

↓

Continue
```

* Producer does **not wait** for any acknowledgement.
* Fastest.
* Highest risk of data loss.

---

## 2. `acks = 1`

```text
Producer

↓

Leader Replica

↓

Ack
```

* Producer waits only for the **Leader Replica**.
* Once the Leader stores the event, it sends an Ack.
* Faster than `acks=all`, but if the Leader fails before Followers copy the event, data may be lost.

---

## 3. `acks = all` (or `acks = -1`)

```text
Producer

↓

Leader

↓

Followers Copy Data

↓

Ack
```

* Producer waits until the required replicas acknowledge the write.
* Safest mode.
* Highest reliability.
* Slightly slower because it waits longer. 

---

# Comparison

| Ack Mode | Waits For         | Speed   | Reliability |
| -------- | ----------------- | ------- | ----------- |
| `0`      | Nobody            | Fastest | Lowest      |
| `1`      | Leader            | Fast    | Medium      |
| `all`    | Required replicas | Slowest | Highest     |

---

# Notes

* An **Ack** is Kafka's confirmation that a Producer's record has been received according to the configured acknowledgement level.
* `acks=0` → No acknowledgement.
* `acks=1` → Wait for the Leader's acknowledgement.
* `acks=all` (`-1`) → Wait for acknowledgements from the required replicas for maximum durability. 

---

# Remember ⭐

* **Ack = Confirmation to the Producer.**
* Higher reliability usually means lower performance.
* **`acks=all` provides the highest durability**, while **`acks=0` provides the highest throughput**.
