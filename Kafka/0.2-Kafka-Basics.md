# Kafka Notes – Part 2 : Kafka Basics (Producer, Consumer, Message Broker, Pub-Sub)

## 1. What is Kafka?

**Definition**

> Kafka is a distributed event streaming platform (messaging system) that enables asynchronous communication between services using the Publish-Subscribe (Pub-Sub) model. It is horizontally scalable, fault-tolerant, and designed for high-throughput messaging. 

---

# 2. Message Broker

## Definition

A **Message Broker** is software that acts as a middleman between services.

Instead of services communicating directly,

they communicate through Kafka.

### Without Kafka

```text
Order Service
      │
      ▼
Inventory Service
```

### With Kafka

```text
Order Service
      │
      ▼
     Kafka
      │
      ▼
Inventory Service
```

Kafka receives messages from producers and delivers them to consumers.

---

# 3. Why Use a Message Broker?

Without Kafka,

one service must know every other service.

```text
Order Service

├── Payment Service
├── Inventory Service
├── Email Service
└── Shipping Service
```

If a new service is added,

Order Service may need additional communication logic.

---

With Kafka,

Order Service communicates only with Kafka.

```text
Order Service
      │
      ▼
     Kafka
      │
      ▼
Other Services
```

New services can start consuming events without modifying the producer.

This reduces **tight coupling**.

---

# 4. Producer

## Definition

A **Producer** is a service/application that publishes events to Kafka.

### Example

Customer places an order.

```text
Customer
     │
     ▼
Order Service
     │
Publishes
OrderPlaced Event
     │
     ▼
Kafka
```

Here,

**Order Service = Producer**

---

Another Example

```text
User Service

↓

Publishes

↓

UserRegistered Event

↓

Kafka
```

The producer only publishes events.

It does **not** know who will consume them.

---

# 5. Consumer

## Definition

A **Consumer** is a service/application that reads events from Kafka and processes them.

### Example

```text
Kafka

↓

Inventory Service
```

Inventory Service consumes

```text
OrderPlaced
```

Then it reduces stock.

Inventory Service is the **Consumer**.

---

Another Example

```text
Kafka

↓

Email Service
```

Consumes

```text
OrderPlaced
```

Then sends an email.

Email Service is also a **Consumer**.

---

# 6. One Producer → Multiple Consumers

A single event can be consumed by multiple services.

```text
               Kafka
                 │
      ┌──────────┼──────────┐
      ▼          ▼          ▼
Inventory    Email     Analytics
Consumer     Consumer   Consumer
```

Example:

Producer publishes:

```text
OrderPlaced
```

Consumers may:

* Inventory Service → Reduce stock
* Email Service → Send confirmation email
* Analytics Service → Record analytics

One event can trigger multiple independent actions.

---

# 7. Publish-Subscribe (Pub-Sub) Model

## Publish

Sending an event to Kafka.

Example:

```text
OrderPlaced
```

is published by Order Service.

---

## Subscribe

Listening for specific events from Kafka.

Example:

Inventory Service subscribes to

```text
OrderPlaced
```

Whenever the event arrives,

Inventory Service processes it.

---

## Pub-Sub Flow

```text
Producer

↓

Publishes Event

↓

Kafka

↓

Consumers
```

Producer publishes.

Consumers subscribe.

They do not communicate directly. 

---

# 8. Complete Flow

```text
Customer Places Order
          │
          ▼
   Order Service
   (Producer)
          │
Publishes OrderPlaced Event
          │
          ▼
        Kafka
 (Message Broker)
          │
   ┌──────┼────────┐
   ▼      ▼        ▼
Inventory Email Analytics
Consumer Consumer Consumer
```

---

# 9. Responsibilities

### Producer

* Creates events
* Publishes events to Kafka
* Doesn't know who consumes the event

---

### Kafka (Message Broker)

* Receives events
* Stores events
* Delivers events to consumers

---

### Consumer

* Reads events
* Processes events independently

---

# 10. Interview Questions

### What is a Producer?

> A Producer is a service that publishes events to Kafka.

---

### What is a Consumer?

> A Consumer is a service that reads events from Kafka and processes them.

---

### What is a Message Broker?

> A Message Broker is software that acts as an intermediary between producers and consumers, receiving and delivering messages.

---

### What is the Publish-Subscribe Model?

> In the Publish-Subscribe model, producers publish events to Kafka, and consumers subscribe to those events to receive and process them.

---

### Key Points to Remember

* Kafka acts as a **Message Broker**.
* Producers **publish** events.
* Consumers **consume** events.
* Producers and consumers are **decoupled**.
* One producer can have **multiple consumers**.
* New consumers can be added without changing the producer.
